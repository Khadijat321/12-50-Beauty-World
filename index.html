About 12:50 Beauty World

Nestled along the bustling Lagos-Badagry Expressway in Ojo, 12:50 Beauty World has established itself as a trusted name in the beauty supply industry. We understand that beauty is personal, and every customer deserves access to quality products that enhance their natural glow.

Our store is stocked with an extensive range of hair extensions, wigs, skincare products, cosmetics, and fragrances from both local Nigerian brands and international labels. Whether you're looking for protective styling options, everyday makeup essentials, or premium hair treatments, our knowledgeable staff is here to guide you.

We pride ourselves on authenticity, affordability, and exceptional customer service. With delivery options available, we bring beauty convenience right to your doorstep.
